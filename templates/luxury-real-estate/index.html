Our Story

Built on relationships that outlast the sale

Thornbury & Hale was founded in 1987 by two brokers who believed a home sale should feel like a conversation, not a transaction. That belief has never changed. Our agents still walk every property in person before it goes to market, still return calls the same day, and still treat a first time buyer with the same care as a family selling the home they raised three children in.

We have worked the same tree lined streets for almost forty years, which means we know which schools are walking distance from which blocks, which renovations add real value, and which homes will sell themselves once the right family walks through the door. Many of our clients today grew up in houses we once sold to their parents, and there is no compliment we take more seriously than that.
